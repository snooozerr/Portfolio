01 / About

Bio + Current Status

Short bio

I enjoy figuring out how things work, questioning assumptions, and approaching problems from different perspectives. I’m drawn to the intersection of science, engineering, and computing, and I believe some of the most interesting ideas emerge when different fields meet. I’d rather understand the fundamentals than settle for knowing just how to use something.

What I am currently doing

Currently pursuing EECS at IISER Bhopal, navigating the fundamentals of computer science and electronics while experimenting with ideas beyond the classroom. I'm strengthening my programming skills, learning digital design and Verilog, working on personal software projects, and exploring cryptography through hands-on experimentation.

Current focus

Particularly interested in hardware security, computer architecture, VLSI, and semiconductor technologies. I want to understand computing at its lowest levels — from logic gates and microarchitecture to the security of the silicon itself. Alongside this, I'm building my problem-solving abilities through competitive programming.
